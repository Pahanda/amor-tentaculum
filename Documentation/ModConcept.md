# Mod Concept
Some time ago, on a very rich Glitterworld, some scientists were working on a project to create a weapon that could be used for covert operations.  They wanted something that would not show up on traditional weapon scanners, so they settled on an artificial, biological lifeform.

The idea is that the lifeform would "infect" a host, and would enhance it in subtle ways.  The Host would have increased strength and durability, as well as built-in armor and weaponry.  Because it was purely biological, it would slip past most security checkpoints.

Their idea worked.  But it worked too well.

Containment broke and the scientists were infected.  The artificial lifeform took over their bodies, but also started to influence their minds, and they began to create other types of Hosts to spread the hive.

One of the scientists suggested that one way to convince people to willingly accept the tentacle infection would be to make it sexually pleasurable.  This echoed back to a more primal phase of humanity; lust and the reproductive urge could override a person's normal resistance to such extreme sexual expressions.

This also worked - better than anyone could possibly have anticipated.  With willing Hosts being eager to spread the tentacles, the hive grew and prospered.

Over time, the hive began to need additional resources and workers.  The remaining scientists engineered independent Tentacle types that could be used to gather or produce resources, defend the hive, and acquire additional biological material.  The consent of said walking biological material (i.e. human beings) became less and less important, and some of the workers and structures that were engineered were deliberately engineered to convert and assimilate unwilling individuals.

At some point, the hive reached sufficient critical mass that some of the Hosts and Tentacles broke off, forming new colonies and spreading among the stars.

Rumors of infested gravships, flesh-filled ruins, and even entire worlds on the Rim - all spreading the infection - began to circulate among starfarers.  Along with rumors of more advanced forms, bringing with them high levels of threats...

You have stumbled onto their research.  And it sounds... kinda kinky, kinda nasty, but also... fun?

Love the Tentacle.  And if others do not?  Convert them - by any means necessary.
